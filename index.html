Privacy Policy — CalCount

Last updated: 2026-10-16

CalCount is a personal calorie logging app.

Data we collect

The app does not create an account and does not send your food or calorie entries to our servers.

Entries and your daily goal are stored only on your device (local storage / app storage).

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email, or login
  • No location tracking for this feature set
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No analytics SDKs (unless listed in a future update to this policy)

Sharing

We do not sell or share your calorie log with third parties, because we do not receive it.

Deletion

Uninstall the app (or clear app storage) to remove local data on that device.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact hello@michaelallanmcmurray.com.

Changes

If the app starts collecting or transmitting personal data, this policy will be updated before that change ships.
